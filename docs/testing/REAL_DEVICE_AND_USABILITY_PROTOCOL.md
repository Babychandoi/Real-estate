# Real-device and usability acceptance protocol

Emulation is pre-release evidence only. Release acceptance requires one physical iPhone/Safari, one lower-cost Android/Chrome, and five representative participants who have not worked on the product.

Record device/OS/browser, viewport/orientation, network, build SHA, consent, task completion, time, errors, assistance, SEQ 1-7 and observations. Do not record CCCD, payment credentials or other PII.

Tasks: find a listing in a drawn map area; compare two listings; create and submit a listing; upload three eKYC images and understand manual review; purchase a plan through the manual VietQR flow; locate the resulting notification. Success target: at least 80% unassisted completion per task, median SEQ at least 5, no critical accessibility or privacy incident.

Test portrait and landscape at 320, 375/390, 768, 1024 and 1440 CSS pixels. Verify touch targets, software keyboard, safe areas, back navigation, zoom to 200%, screen reader landmarks and slow-network recovery. Attach screenshots/video only with participant consent and sign the result; an empty form is not a passed usability test.
