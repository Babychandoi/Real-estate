## Brand & Style

This design system targets modern Vietnamese property seekers, brokers, and investors navigating an increasingly digital and fast-paced real estate ecosystem. The visual language conveys institutional trust, legal clarity, and certified authenticity while shedding the dense, outdated clutter typical of legacy Vietnamese listing platforms.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate** with **Soft High-Trust Tactility**:
- **Clarity and Assurance:** Generous breathing room, crisp typographic hierarchy, and clear transactional cues provide buyers confidence regarding legal integrity and verified ownership.
- **Mobile-First Real Estate Utility:** Tailored specifically for fast on-the-go scanning on standard handheld displays (390px base width), highlighting key transactional metadata: unit pricing (VND/m²), legal status (Sổ hồng/Sổ đỏ), verification badges, and direct broker contact actions (Gọi, Zalo, Chat).
- **Subtle Modernism:** Smooth rounded corners, soft ambient depth layers, and purposeful semantic color accents that separate certified records from unverified market noise.

## Colors

The color palette establishes immediate legal authority and effortless scanning through high-contrast, purpose-driven hues:

- **Primary (`#0F4C81` - Royal Sapphire Navy):** Anchors headers, primary navigation, core brand elements, and authoritative legal indicators. Represents transparency, legal stability, and institutional trust.
- **Secondary / Verified Accent (`#059669` - Emerald Green):** Dedicated strictly to authenticity markers—verified badges ("Đã xác thực", "Đã duyệt"), confirmed owner phone numbers, and approved property papers.
- **Tertiary / Market Accent (`#D97706` - Warm Amber Gold):** Reserved for high-value distinctions, VIP listing banners, price reductions, and urgent notifications.
- **Conversion Accent (`#0284C7` - Vibrant Sky Blue):** High-salience operational color for primary search filters, interactive map markers, and high-priority lead conversion buttons ("Liên hệ ngay", "Đặt lịch xem").
- **Neutral & Background Tones:**
  - Canvas Base: `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) and `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) providing soft, glare-free contrast against pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card surfaces.
  - Text Hierarchy: Deep Ink (`#0F172A`) for headlines and prices, Slate Gray (`#475569`) for metadata, and Muted Tint (`#94A3B8`) for borders and structural dividers.

## Typography

The type system relies entirely on **Be Vietnam Pro**, engineered natively for optimal diacritic rendering of Vietnamese tone marks (hỏi, ngã, nặng, sắc, huyền) without irregular baseline shifts or cramped accents.

- **Numerics & Currency:** Vietnamese property values often involve multi-digit billions (e.g., "5,25 tỷ", "42 triệu/m²"). Price tokens use heavier weights (`FontWeight 700`) with tighter letter tracking (`-0.02em`) to guarantee quick recognition in dense listing feeds.
- **Mobile Adaptations:** Scale down large headlines by 20–25% on viewports narrower than 640px to prevent awkward Vietnamese word-wrapping (e.g., keeping terms like "Bất động sản" or "Quận Bình Thạnh" intact across line breaks).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an adaptable 12-column grid for desktop (max-width 1280px) and a continuous single/dual-column fluid grid for mobile devices:

- **Mobile Viewports (390px base):**
  - Edge Margins: Fixed at `16px` (`margin-mobile`), ensuring maximal utilization of mobile real estate without touching physical bezels.
  - Column Gutters: `12px` (`gutter-mobile`) for 2-column card layouts (e.g., quick filter grids, image galleries).
- **Rhythm & Padding:**
  - Component interior spacing strictly follows the 4px baseline, relying heavily on `space-sm` (8px) between icon-text couples, `space-md` (16px) for interior card body padding, and `space-lg` (24px) for distinct card section stacking.
- **Reflow Architecture:**
  - Desktop: 3-column feed with sticky side filters and floating map split-screen.
  - Mobile: Full-width stacked listing cards, horizontal scrollable chips for fast filtering, and a persistent bottom action dock for lead conversion (Call/Zalo).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses layered tonal contrast combined with diffused, low-opacity ambient shadows tinted with the primary hue (`#0F4C81`):

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface):** Canvas background (`#F8FAFC`). No elevation, flat structure.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Listings):** Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), crisp border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, and ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(15, 76, 129, 0.06), 0px 1px 4px -1px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** Smooth vertical lift: `0px 10px 20px -4px rgba(15, 76, 129, 0.10), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Lead Dock & Modals):** High-priority floating layers (Mobile bottom navigation bar, quick inquiry drawer): `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 76, 129, 0.15), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)`.
- **Backdrop Frost (Search & Floating Bars):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` for headers and sticky sub-navigation to maintain spatial awareness during long scrolls.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, modern geometric curvature (`roundedness: 2` base):

- **Listing Cards & Modals:** Standardized on `rounded-xl` (16px) to `rounded-2xl` (20px) to project a soft, welcoming, and modern lifestyle feel.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons):** Standardized on `rounded-lg` (8px to 10px), maintaining geometric stability and clarity for data entry.
- **Status Badges & Category Chips:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to contrast with rectangular property photo containers and data tables.
- **Media Containers:** Upper edges of listing cards adhere to the card radius (`rounded-t-xl` / 16px), with inner badges inset by `8px` or `12px`.

## Components

### 1. Listing Cards (Card Bất Động Sản)
- **Structure:** 16:10 aspect ratio image carousel on top, followed by structured metadata below.
- **Photo Overlays:** 
  - Top-Left: Dual pill tags for transaction type ("Bán" / "Cho thuê" in Royal Navy `#0F4C81` or Emerald `#059669`).
  - Top-Right: Favorite icon button (circular white surface with soft drop-shadow).
  - Bottom-Left: Image count indicator (translucent black pill, e.g., "1/8").
- **Body Content:**
  - Price prominently displayed in Primary Navy or Deep Ink (`price-display`), followed by unit rate (`45 tr/m²`).
  - Single-line title with high contrast, followed by 3 key metrics separated by dividers: Area (m²), Bedrooms (PN), Bathrooms (WC).
  - Footer: Broker avatar, verification badge ("Đã kiểm tra SĐT"), and post age ("Hôm nay").

### 2. Status Chips & Verification Badges
- **Verified ("Đã xác thực / Đã duyệt"):** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#047857`, border `1px solid #A7F3D0`, prepended by a checkmark shield icon.
- **Transaction Mode ("Bán / Cho thuê"):** Neutral tint `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`, font weight 600.
- **VIP Listing ("Tin nổi bật"):** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, prepended by a star icon.

### 3. Buttons & Conversion Calls
- **Primary CTA ("Liên hệ ngay / Gọi điện"):** Solid Sky Blue (`#0284C7`) or Royal Sapphire (`#0F4C81`), text white, `height: 48px` on mobile for ergonomic thumb tapping, `rounded-lg`.
- **Secondary Zalo Action:** Solid `#0068FF` (official Zalo blue) or soft tint `#E0F2FE` with blue text, displaying the verified messaging icon.
- **Filter Pills:** Outline `1px solid #E2E8F0`, active state filled with `#0F4C81` and text white.

### 4. Inputs & Search Fields
- **Master Search Bar:** Integrated pill container or `rounded-xl` block with high interior padding (`14px 16px`), left-aligned location search icon, right-aligned quick filter modal trigger.
- **Form Inputs:** Border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, focus ring `2px solid #0284C7` with `0 0 0 4px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`.

### 5. Mobile Fixed Lead Bar (Thanh Chốt Khách Mobile)
- Pinned to the bottom of the screen on listing detail pages: 64px height, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)` with backdrop blur, dual split layout featuring:
  - Left (40%): Direct Call button with broker photo and "Gọi điện".
  - Right (60%): "Chat Zalo / Nhắn tin" button in vibrant accent tone for instantaneous response.
